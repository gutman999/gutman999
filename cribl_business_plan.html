Cribl Business Plan

Scale Cribl into the default telemetry data routing and optimization layer for enterprise observability and security, enabling organizations to control data cost while improving reliability and analytics quality.

1) Executive Summary

Cribl delivers a unified data pipeline for logs, metrics, traces, and security events. The company reduces ingestion and storage costs, improves data quality, and accelerates incident response by routing the right data to the right destination in real time.

$100M+ ARR Target within 3 years
120%+ NRR expansion-led growth
40%+ FCF Margin at mature scale

2) Problem and Opportunity

  • Telemetry volumes are growing 20-40% annually, driving unsustainable observability and SIEM costs.
  • Teams struggle with fragmented tooling and duplicate pipelines across security and operations.
  • Compliance needs require selective retention and policy-driven redaction before storage.
  • Enterprises need vendor-neutral control over data flows to avoid lock-in and optimize spend.

3) Product and Value Proposition

  • Cribl Stream: route, transform, enrich, sample, and redact data in real time.
  • Cribl Edge: collect and process telemetry close to source for lower transport cost.
  • Cribl Search: search data in place across object stores and observability backends.
  • Cribl Lake: cost-efficient long-term telemetry lake with governance and access controls.

Primary customer value: 30-70% telemetry cost reduction with better investigative signal quality.

4) Target Market

  • Primary ICP: enterprise and upper-midmarket with annual observability spend above $500K.
  • Personas: platform engineering, SRE leaders, SOC leaders, and cloud cost owners.
  • Vertical focus: financial services, healthcare, SaaS, and public sector.
  • TAM thesis: data routing layer becomes standard in every multi-tool telemetry stack.

5) Go-to-Market Strategy

  • Land with one high-cost telemetry workload and prove savings within 30-60 days.
  • Expand from logs into security, metrics, traces, and compliance use cases.
  • Partner ecosystem: hyperscalers, MSSPs, SI partners, and observability vendors.
  • Technical content engine: benchmarking, architecture guides, and customer ROI playbooks.

6) Operating Model and Milestones

  • Product-led proof-of-value with reusable deployment templates and policy packs.
  • Quarterly release cadence focused on governance, AI-assisted pipelines, and data quality SLAs.
  • Customer success aligned to measurable savings and reduced MTTR outcomes.

8) Risks and Mitigations

  • Platform overlap risk: defend with open integrations and fastest time-to-value.
  • Procurement pressure: package clear ROI and consumption governance guarantees.
  • Execution complexity: standardize onboarding via blueprints and managed services.
  • Data governance risk: invest in policy controls, auditing, and enterprise certifications.
